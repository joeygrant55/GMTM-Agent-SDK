## Athlete session prompts

1. “Show me where you are with your combine today.” Observe their existing workflow and compare their understanding with the source records.
2. “Using SPARQ, show me what you would do next.” Note wrong athlete/division, unclear progress, dead ends and facilitator intervention.
3. “Choose one unfinished requirement and show me how you would work on it.” If genuine media or results are ready, the athlete may complete their normal GMTM submission and return to SPARQ. Otherwise follow up when they naturally submit; a short session need not include filming/testing.
4. “Find help for something you're unsure about.” Observe whether they find the help control, whether the answer is grounded in the actual requirement, and whether they can act on it.
5. For a completed athlete: “Show me what this says about your combine now. What are you trying to do next, and how are you doing that today?” Record unmet needs as research; coach sharing, verified selection outcomes and broader recruiting services are not implemented promises in this candidate.

Ask participants to think aloud. Record observed behavior before offering guidance. Obtain permission before recording; use deidentified notes without session tokens, claim links or other athletes' data. Participation and feedback should be presented as optional; coordinate the wording with Charles so no selection benefit is implied.

## Session record and decision

Use one row per participant: anonymous label; starting completion stage; phone/browser; environment/build; intended event; steps completed independently; time to first correct next action; facilitator minutes and nature of intervention; observed defect; genuine source save and matching refresh if exercised; help answer issue; next real task. Mark fixture, live observation and participant report distinctly. Timings describe these sessions and support effort; they are not population estimates.

Stop expansion for any wrong-person/wrong-division data, ownership error, lost submission, or materially incorrect completion claim. Fix repeated blockers before the next round. The technical acceptance needs at least one observed genuine GMTM save → matching SPARQ refresh; that alone does not prove completion uplift.

With permission, a short follow-up after two or three days can ask what the athlete actually completed and whether they returned. Distinguish a return they initiated from one prompted by our message. Use those accounts to guide iteration. Once the flow is stable, separately design a larger completion-rate comparison with a defined eligible cohort, observation window and comparison group before making revenue/customer outcome claims.
